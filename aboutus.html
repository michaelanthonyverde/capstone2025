About This Project

This site is Group 2's AI-powered GRE vocabulary capstone project, built around a curated list of test-ready words. It uses adaptive hints guided by a Q-learning approach to respond to how you study and what you miss. The goal is to keep feedback quick, focused, and transparent so you can move through words with confidence.

Project Goals

  • Deliver focused GRE vocabulary practice with curated difficulty.
  • Surface adaptive hints that adjust to each learner's patterns.
  • Keep the experience fast, clean, and distraction-free.
  • Make progress easy to pick up across sessions and devices.

How the Quiz Works

Each quiz pulls balanced words across difficulty levels and tracks your correct choices. As you answer, the Q-learning hint logic nudges you with brief cues that favor gaps in your history, so the hints get sharper the more you practice.

Sponsor Collaboration

Our adaptive hint model started as a Python engine provided by the sponsor. We reimplemented that Q-learning flow in JavaScript so everything runs directly in the browser without a backend dependency.

Future Improvements

  • Audio pronunciations and richer examples.
  • Progress dashboards with streaks and mastery levels.
  • Offline-friendly study mode.
